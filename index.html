How it works

From your pincode to the final.

Your pincode is your squad name. It goes on the scoreboard, the player introductions, the squad shirts and every post about your team.

01

Register by pincode

Sign up under the pincode where you live. Players from the same area are grouped together.

02

Join your squad

We group players from the same area into neighbourhood squads of 4 to 6. Know players from your pincode? Ask them to sign up too.

Every squad includes one junior (under 16).

03

Play for your pincode

Squads meet in team ties across five categories. Win your group, then it's knockout all the way to the final.

Amateurs only. No professional players, no coaches in the draw.Squad shirt included* with every registration.

Questions

Before you sign up

Do I need to have a team already?

No. Sign up on your own and we build squads by pincode. Friends from the same area can each register, and we'll keep you together where we can.

When and where is it?

Tentatively 23–24 January 2027 at AITA Courts, Kalina, Mumbai.* Final dates and venue are confirmed closer to the event, based on entries and venue availability.

What proof of residence do I need?

Any document showing your name and an address in your pincode, such as Aadhaar, a rental agreement or a utility bill. We may ask to see it before squads are confirmed.

Will matches be streamed?

Yes. The show courts are streamed live with commentary, including the semi-finals and the final.

I don't want to compete. Can I still come?

Yes. The fun fair is open to everyone all day: the One-Point Shootout, serve-speed and target challenges, fitness games, a kids' zone and stalls.
