How it works

Two ways in. One route to the final.

Your pincode is your squad name. It goes on the scoreboard, the player introductions, the squad shirts and every post about your team.

01

Register by pincode

Sign up under the pincode where you live. Players from the same area are grouped together.

02

Join or bring a squad

Enter on your own and we'll place you in your neighbourhood squad, or enter as a ready-made squad of 4 to 6 players from the same area.

Every squad includes one junior (under 16).

03

Play for your pincode

Squads meet in team ties across five categories. Win your group, then it's knockout all the way to the final.

Amateurs only. No professional players, no coaches in the draw.Squad shirt included with every registration.

Questions

Before you sign up

Do I need to have a team already?

No. Most players enter on their own and we build squads by pincode. If you already have 4 to 6 players from the same area, you can enter together.

Is there an entry fee?

Yes. The fee is announced when registration opens, and it includes your squad playing shirt. Registering interest now is free.

When and where is it?

A two-day weekend in Mumbai. Dates and venue go out first to everyone who registers interest.

Will matches be streamed?

Yes. The show courts are streamed live with commentary, including the semi-finals and the final.

I don't want to compete. Can I still come?

Yes. The fun fair is open to everyone all day: the One-Point Shootout, serve-speed and target challenges, fitness games, a kids' zone and stalls. Tick "Visit the fun fair" in the form.
